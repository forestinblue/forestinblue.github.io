Range-aware uncertainty calibration in self-supervised monocular depth

Jun Seok Kim, Rui Huang

The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen


The scale of a learned depth uncertainty can be re-measured without labels where the model is used: one constant from stereo-matching residuals reads the ground-truth level of σ to within ×1.08 in 27 of 28 readings across networks and datasets, and with it a self-supervised model's worst-range miscalibration drops by 86%.


Problem. Systems that consume a depth uncertainty σ read it as a number, and those that state how they set its scale fit a constant with labels. Self-supervised depth is built for places without labels, yet its σ is scored almost only by ranking, which cannot see the scale. On KITTI, released MonoProb under-reports its error in all five depth ranges; in the nearest range the typical error is 3.7× what σ implies. Retraining on self-supervised signals alone never beat a single ground-truth-fitted global scale (0 of 44 runs).


Finding. The right constant differs by network and by dataset: by one label-free rule it ranges from ×1.81 to ×2.46 across four KITTI-trained networks, and the KITTI constant carried to nuScenes leaves all five ranges under-reported. Measured by that rule on the same images, a constant from stereo-matching residuals lies within ×1.08 of the ground-truth level in 27 of 28 σ readings on KITTI, Argoverse 1 and DrivingStereo (the exception is Metric3D v2 in its native metric frame), needs no more images than a LiDAR-fitted constant, and on the published σ of two other systems reads their own ground-truth level within 0.11 nats on full-resolution KITTI input.


Figure 1 makes this visible. Each point groups pixels with similar predicted uncertainty σ and plots their typical actual error. Points on the line are calibrated: their typical error is what σ predicts. MonoProb's grey points all lie above it: its uncertainty is too small, most of all where it is most confident. Ours, in blue, moves onto the line for most groups; its two most confident groups stay above it and its least confident sit slightly below. Under a mean-square criterion, which weights rare large errors more, the gap narrows from 3.6× to 1.2× but does not close.


Figure 1. Released MonoProb base model (ResNet-18, 640×192) and ours, mean of 4 runs, on our KITTI validation split.

Limits. Label-free, not sensor-free: stereo pairs are needed once per deployment domain, and the constant is measured on the pixel population on which σ is evaluated (on KITTI the benchmark crop; elsewhere the positions of LiDAR returns, no value read); without stereo, as on nuScenes, it cannot be re-measured. One constant fixes the level, not the shape: with a Dynamo-Depth target, and for the recipe retrained from scratch on DrivingStereo, σ loses to the ground-truth-fitted scale, and on the full Argoverse 1 and DrivingStereo frames it loses in 2 of 4 runs each.
