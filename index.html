Research Projects

Jun Seok Kim

I am an MSc student in Artificial Intelligence and Robotics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, advised by Prof. Rui Huang. Before that, I received bachelor's degrees in Information Statistics and Data Science from Kangwon National University and worked as a data scientist at Connple.

I want robots to know when their own perception should not be trusted. My research interests are uncertainty calibration for learned perception and 3D scene representation for robotics, including perception under compute and communication constraints and real-time reconstruction of dynamic scenes.

I also founded TARS, a foot-traffic analytics service that runs monocular depth estimation on edge cameras for over 60 small businesses.

I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

Jun Seok Kim, Rui Huang

Preprint, 2026. Under review.

LocalAggTR: Differentiable Voxelization for Weakly Supervised 3D Occupancy

Capstone project, 2025. Awarded Distinction.

A differentiable Local Aggregation operator replaces the Gaussian-to-voxel step of GaussTR, making inference 7.2× faster with mIoU unchanged on nuScenes.
