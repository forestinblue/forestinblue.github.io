Range-Aware Uncertainty Calibration in Self-Supervised Monocular Depth

MSc thesis, 2026. Paper in preparation with Prof. Rui Huang.

Robots act on the uncertainty of self-supervised depth models, yet it is evaluated only by ranking, so its magnitude goes unchecked. I show that self-supervised retraining alone cannot close this gap, while a single label-free scale constant estimated from stereo residuals can. This cuts worst-range calibration error by 89% compared with the original model, using no ground-truth depth.

LocalAggTR: Differentiable Voxelization for Weakly Supervised 3D Occupancy

Capstone project, 2025. Awarded Distinction.

The Gaussian-to-voxel step in GaussTR is non-differentiable and dominates inference time. I replaced it with a differentiable Local Aggregation operator that passes occupancy gradients, making inference 7.2× faster with mIoU unchanged on nuScenes. Adopted by the lab's 3D Gaussian Splatting project for semi-supervised fine-tuning.

Point Cloud Density and 3D Object Detection

2025.

How does a 3D detector degrade when LiDAR density drops, and can upsampling recover it? With PointPillars on nuScenes-mini, voxel-grid thinning to one fifth of the points cost 2.1% of mAP, while random thinning to half cost about 4× more.

Geometric vs. Generative 3D Augmentation for Point Cloud Classification

2025.

Does text-to-3D generative augmentation help a point cloud classifier more than geometric augmentation? With PointNet on ModelNet40, geometric augmentation raised accuracy by 6.5 points, while text-to-3D generative augmentation did not help.
